Kings & Prophets

Kings, prophets, priests and world powers of Israel and Judah, year by year, from 1051 to 430 BC. Every name carries the verse it comes from. You can switch between two scholars' chronologies, Thiele's and Galil's. Their tables were read from online reproductions, not from the original books.

931 BC
drag the slider, or jump to a reign

What the two chronologies are. Thiele and Galil are two published reconstructions of the same regnal numbers in Kings and Chronicles. They differ by up to about twenty years in the eighth century, so the same year can put a different king on the throne. Where a table gives overlapping years, the app marks it as a co-regency, a rival reign, or simply an overlap the source does not explain. Nothing here is averaged or smoothed.

What is approximate. Prophets' ranges are envelopes drawn from the reigns their books name; they are labelled "approx." and are not claims about when each one began or stopped. High priests have no dates at all in this app, because the text gives them none. Saul, David and Solomon under Thiele, and Saul under Galil, have derived dates: worked out from the verses, not taken from a table (each card shows the arithmetic). Thiele's dates for Hoshea and Galil's dates for David and Solomon come from Wikipedia's columns alone, not from the reproduced tables.

Split years. A year written like 931/930 is one regnal year that ran across two of our calendar years, because the ancient year did not begin in January. The slider places it at the earlier of the two.

Borders are not drawn, on purpose. The map is coastline, two lakes, four rivers and place markers.

Counts in this build: 43 kings and rulers, 28 prophets placed (5 the text does not date), 16 priests, 29 events, 39 places.
